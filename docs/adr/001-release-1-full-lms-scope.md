# ADR-001: Release-1 Full LMS Scope

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-08-04

## Decision

Release 1 includes tagged content/PDFs, assignments/submissions, quizzes/question banks, gradebook/marking/feedback, deadlines/calendar, announcements/messaging, SCORM/LTI, parent access, MIS/SIS sync, Moodle migration, and compliance reporting.

## Consequences

The product is a full LMS programme rather than a content-portal MVP. Each domain requires separate contracts, threat review, owned work packages, tests, and integration gates. “Release 1” does not permit unsafe simultaneous coding: internal increments and pilot gates remain mandatory even though all capabilities are required before general launch.
